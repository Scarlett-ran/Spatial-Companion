# Exploration 02 — Product Thesis and Validation Framework

## 1. Core thesis

> **Baobao has a life of his own. Sometimes, that life intersects with yours.**

Baobao is a harp seal pup. When the user arrives, he is already resting or observing his surroundings. They may share a brief interaction, or simply continue with their own activities. The product explores connection through quiet co-presence, mutual awareness, and lightweight interaction while preserving the user's freedom to leave.

| Principle | Meaning in this project |
|---|---|
| Autonomous | Baobao has his own activities and rhythm; the user's arrival does not force an immediate response. |
| Spatial | He should have a credible position, scale, and approachable body. |
| Companion | Connection grows through co-presence and occasional encounters. |
| No care obligations | Feeding, check-ins, punishment, and relationship maintenance do not control the user's return. |

A small set of preset activities can express autonomy. “Noticing the user” is currently an experience goal, not identity recognition, long-term memory, or a simulated life running in the background.

## 2. Three independent value questions

| Level | Question |
|---|---|
| Presence | Does Baobao feel present in the user's space? Contact credibility is assessed separately. |
| Relationship | Does he provide companionship during active and passive moments without creating pressure? |
| Product | Is that value strong enough to justify the device conditions, repeated use, and a particular product form? |

The project currently has two stages. I will first build and refine the Browser Demo independently, then use the mature result to seek on-device access and external support. Self-testing establishes whether the simulated logic works; user understanding requires feedback from other people. On-device work begins with spatial presence. Passive co-presence can follow if presence shows promise, while contact remains an optional, separate experiment. Short co-presence, repeated encounters, and adoption are assessed independently. See [05 — Assumption Map](./05_Assumption_Map.md).

## 3. Scope and long-term direction

| Scope | Content |
|---|---|
| Current Browser stage | Resting, observing, noticing, one invited response, a natural ending, and hide/pause/recovery controls. |
| First on-device round | Review the platform, establish credible scale and stable placement, and test approach and viewing from different angles. |
| Later, evidence-led options | Contact, co-presence, repeated encounters, adoption, a world, objects, memory, growth, and cross-app visits. |
| Incompatible with the thesis | Mandatory feeding, check-ins, hunger upkeep, punishment, relationship decay, intimacy maintenance, or offline care debt. |

The long-term idea is **Baobao's world ↔ Baobao visits ↔ the user's digital world**. It does not imply permanent presence across every app and is outside the current build.
