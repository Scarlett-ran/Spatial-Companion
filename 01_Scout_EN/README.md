# 01 — Exploration Phase (Scout)

Scout centers on a single primary question: **Real pet ownership is constrained by time, energy, money, housing conditions, and long-term responsibility, while 2D virtual pets lack a sense of embodiment in a space shared with the user. Can a spatial pet address both limitations?** Low disruption is a design constraint, and Baobao is the established character. Companionship effects, acceptance among young users, and technical feasibility remain assumptions.

Begin with the top-level [`Product Roadmap`](../00_Product_Roadmap.md) for the six-stage path, then read [`00_Exploration_Overview.md`](./00_Exploration_Overview.md) for the evidence and boundaries of the current stage.

The overview remains the source of truth for current outputs and validation status.
